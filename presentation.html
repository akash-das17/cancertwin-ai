Problem Statement: The 4 Crises in Oncology Care
Why cancer decision support cannot rely on manual consultation or naive single-prompt LLMs
CLINICAL CRISES
1. Fragmented Oncology Data Silos
ISOLATED EHRs, PATHOLOGY & GENOMICS

Patient records, pathology slides, blood panels, and genetic biomarkers (BRCA1/2, KRAS, Lynch) reside in disconnected hospital systems. Oncologists spend 40%+ of patient review time manually gathering history, leading to missed diagnostic windows.

2. 18+ Variable Cognitive Overload
COMPLEX MULTI-AXIS HAZARD MATH

Calculating 10-to-30-year risk divergence requires simultaneously synthesizing lifestyle exposures, environmental carcinogens (PM2.5, radon), genetics, and comorbidity indices. Computing Gail, Bach, or Cox hazards manually during a 15-minute consultation is impossible.

3. Fatal Drug Collisions & Regimen Burnout
UNCHECKED DDI & HIGH CLINICAL FRICTION

Aggressive chemo/chemoprevention regimens trigger fatal Adverse Drug Reactions (e.g. Tamoxifen collided with CYP2D6-inhibiting antidepressants). Demanding 10 simultaneous lifestyle modifications at once causes high friction scores and 70%+ patient dropout.

4. General LLM Hallucination Trap
UNVERIFIED CITATIONS & BLAND AVERAGES

Commercial monolithic LLMs invent realistic-sounding PubMed PMIDs, fail token budgeting, and converge on generic averages rather than exploring conflicting surgical resection versus radiation field margins. In cancer care, ungrounded AI is unacceptable.
